About this section

The first years of a career are shaped by a few decisions: which internships to pursue, how to present your experience and what a realistic path from intern to full-time analyst looks like. These guides explain each stage in plain language, from the sophomore-year internship search to your first full-time offer.

Where to start

  • Read From Intern to Analyst for a timeline of the recruiting cycle.
  • See the best US companies for finance internships and the top finance specializations to decide where to aim.
  • Learn how to pay for college with paid internships and co-ops.

When you are ready to apply, build a clean document in the CV Builder and check it with the ATS Resume Analyzer. Salary and hiring information changes, so treat the figures in our guides as starting points and confirm current details with employers and official sources.
