ABOUT WELLS LOVE

Wells Love Editorial Team

Guides on Wells Love are written and reviewed by our editorial team, which covers higher education, personal finance and early-career recruiting for students and young professionals.

Focus areas

  • University selection and admissions across the US, UK and Europe
  • College costs, student loans and return on investment
  • Internships, CVs, applicant tracking systems and entry-level finance careers

How a guide is produced

  1. Pick a question students actually ask and define who the answer is for.
  2. Gather facts from primary sources such as government statistics, university sites and official ranking publishers.
  3. Write in plain language, state assumptions and mark editorial opinion as opinion.
  4. Review, date and republish whenever figures change.

Corrections

Found an error or outdated number? Write to contact@Wells Love with the page and the source. Verified corrections are made promptly and the review date is updated.
