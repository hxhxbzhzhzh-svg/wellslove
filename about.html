ABOUT WELLS LOVE

About Wells Love

Wells Love is an independent education and career resource for students and early-career professionals. We combine free browser-based tools with plain-language guides on choosing a university, understanding what a degree really costs, and building a first career in finance and technology.

Why this site exists

Choosing where to study, how to pay for it and how to present yourself to employers are some of the most expensive decisions young people make, and much of the advice online is either sales copy or buried in jargon. We build tools that let you test the numbers with your own figures, and we write guides that explain the reasoning so you can check it.

What we publish

  • Tools: a College ROI Calculator, a Loan Calculator, a CV Builder and an ATS Resume Analyzer that accepts PDF, Word or pasted text.
  • Guides: university shortlists, career-path timelines and finance specializations. See the article library.

Who writes it

Content is produced by the wellslovehub.com and published under the standards in our editorial policy. The tools are designed and built in-house.

Privacy by design

The CV Builder and ATS Analyzer run in your browser. Resume files you upload are read on your device and are not sent to our servers. Details are in the Privacy Policy.

How we are funded

The site is free to use. We may display advertising or earn from partner links, which never changes how we rank or describe a school, employer or product. See the Advertiser Disclosure.

What we are not

We are not a financial adviser, lawyer, admissions consultant or recruiter. Tool results are estimates for planning, not guarantees. Contact us at contact@wellslovehub.com with questions or corrections.
