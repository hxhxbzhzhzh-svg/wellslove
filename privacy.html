Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026.

This policy explains what information wellslovehub.com ("Wells Love", "we", "us") collects when you visit the site, how it is used, and the choices you have. By using the site you agree to the practices described here.

Information we collect

We collect only what is needed to run and improve the site: (1) technical data that your browser sends automatically, such as IP address, device and browser type, pages viewed and referring page; (2) usage data from analytics tools, such as which pages are popular and how long visits last; and (3) any information you choose to send us by email, such as a question or correction request. We do not require an account and we do not ask for payment details.

Tools run in your browser

The College ROI Calculator, Loan Calculator, Pomodoro Timer, CV Builder and ATS Resume Analyzer process the numbers and text you enter inside your own browser. Resume files you upload to the ATS Resume Analyzer are read on your device and are not uploaded to our servers. Please do not enter information you would not want on a shared computer.

Cookies and similar technologies

We and our partners use cookies and similar technologies for essential functions, for measuring traffic and for advertising. Our Cookie Policy lists the categories and explains how to control them.

Advertising and Google AdSense

We use Google AdSense to display advertising. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your previous visits to this site and other sites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the internet. Where required by law, we ask for your consent before personalized advertising is used.

You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, or by visiting aboutads.info. You can learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. We do not control the cookies set by third parties, and you should review their privacy policies.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the site. It collects information such as pages viewed and approximate location. You can prevent this by using the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on or by blocking cookies in your browser.

How we use information

We use information to operate and secure the site, to understand which content and tools are useful, to answer messages, to display advertising and to meet legal obligations. We do not sell personal information in exchange for money.

Sharing

We share information only with service providers that help us run the site (for example hosting, analytics and advertising partners), when required by law, or to protect the site and its users. These providers may process data in countries other than your own.

Retention

Server logs and analytics data are kept for as long as needed for the purposes above and then deleted or anonymized. Emails to us are kept while needed to answer you and for reasonable record-keeping.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR, the UK GDPR, or the California CCPA/CPRA), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information, to object to or restrict certain processing, to withdraw consent and to complain to your data protection authority. To make a request, email privacy@wellslovehub.com. We may need to verify your identity before acting on it.

Children

The site is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

External links

Articles link to universities, government agencies and other sites. We are not responsible for the content or privacy practices of those sites.

Changes

We may update this policy as the site or the law changes. The date at the top shows the latest revision.
