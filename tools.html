Which tool should I use?

  • Choosing a university or major: start with the College Cost & ROI Calculator to compare total cost with expected starting pay, then read the guide on the 11 expenses of college.
  • Borrowing to pay for it: use the Loan Calculator to see the monthly payment and total interest, and read Student Loans in 2026 first.
  • Applying for internships and jobs: build a clean document in the CV Builder and test it with the ATS Resume Analyzer.
  • Publishing your own website or blog: paste a page into the SEO Checker to catch missing titles, headings, canonical tags and structured data.
  • Presenting a project or thesis: pick a design from 4,800 free presentation templates, change the text and download the .pptx.
  • Staying focused while you study: use the Pomodoro Timer.

How our tools are built

The tools use simplified, documented assumptions and run entirely on your device. Results are estimates for planning and are not financial or career advice. If you notice a problem, write to support@wellslovehub.com.

What each tool is for

Presentation Templates offers thousands of editable PowerPoint designs for school, work and research, with backgrounds for subjects from business and medicine to history and aviation. College Cost & ROI Calculator adds up eleven kinds of college expense, subtracts aid and compares the total with expected earnings. Loan Calculator shows what a loan will cost each month and over its whole life. Pomodoro Timer structures study time into focused sessions and breaks. CV Builder and ATS Resume Analyzer help you write a clear resume and test it against a job description. SEO Checker reviews the title, headings, links and structured data of a web page for people who publish their own sites.

Privacy

The tools run in your browser. The numbers you type, the files you open and the documents you build are processed on your own device and are not sent to our servers. See the Privacy Policy for details about analytics and advertising cookies.
