US resume, UK CV or Europass?

The same career story is presented differently by country. A US resume is usually one page for early-career candidates, omits a photo, date of birth and marital status, and leads with results. A UK CV is often up to two pages and may include a short personal profile. Europass is a standardized European format that some public-sector and international employers prefer. Choose the format that matches the country and the employer's instructions.

Writing tips that help

  • Start each bullet with a strong verb and state the result, for example "Reduced reporting time by building an Excel template used by five analysts."
  • Quantify when you honestly can, and never invent figures or titles.
  • Use the language of the job description for skills you really have.
  • Keep fonts and layout simple so recruiting software can read them.
  • Proofread twice and ask someone else to read it.
