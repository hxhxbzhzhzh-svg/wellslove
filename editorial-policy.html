Editorial Policy

Accuracy and sourcing

Statistics and factual claims come from primary or authoritative sources, listed at the end of each guide. When we cite a ranking we name its publisher. Lists marked "editorial" are our own judgment, not a scored index.

Independence

Advertisers and partners do not influence which schools, employers or products we mention or how we describe them. Sponsored or affiliate links, if any, are disclosed.

Updates

Each guide shows when it was published and last reviewed. Tuition, rankings, salaries and policies change, so we revisit guides at least once a year.

Tools and estimates

Calculators and analyzers use simplified, documented assumptions. Results are estimates, not financial, legal or hiring advice.

Corrections

Send corrections to contact@wellslovehub.com. We verify against the source, fix the page and update the review date.
